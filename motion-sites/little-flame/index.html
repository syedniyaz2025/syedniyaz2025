How I make them

Slow on purpose.

  1. Thursday evening

    Melt

    Soy flakes go into the double boiler and come up to 85 degrees. Any hotter and the scent burns off.

  2. Thursday, later

    Blend

    Oils are weighed to the gram and stirred in for two full minutes. I time it. Every batch.

  3. Friday morning

    Pour

    Twenty-four jars, wicks centred by hand, poured in one go so every candle sets the same.

  4. Two weeks on the shelf

    Cure

    They sit and do nothing. It's the boring step that makes them fill a room.
