How it works

Four visits. No surprises.

  1. i

    Home visit

    We measure, listen and photograph. An hour, free, no brochure.

  2. ii

    Design

    Hand drawings, a 3D walk-through and a fixed, itemised price.

  3. iii

    Make

    Eight weeks in our workshop. Visit whenever you like.

  4. iv

    Fit

    Ten working days on site, dust sheets down, kettle on.
